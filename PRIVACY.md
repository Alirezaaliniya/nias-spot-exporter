# Privacy Policy — nias spot exporter

_Last updated: 2026-11-06_

**nias spot exporter** does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

- **What it reads:** only when you click the extension on `panel.spotplayer.ir`, it reads the course
  list and the titles/durations of course items shown on that page, or — on the licenses page —
  the license rows shown there (license holder name, tags, device/usage counters, dates, and
  device platform/IP when a row is expanded). This is your own panel data.
- **Where data goes:** nowhere. The extracted data is stored locally in your browser
  (`chrome.storage.local`) so you can keep editing it. Your design preferences are stored in
  `chrome.storage.sync` (synced by Chrome to your own Google account if Chrome sync is on).
- **No network requests:** the extension makes no requests to any server, uses no analytics,
  and loads no remote code.
- **Exports:** Word/TXT/JSON files are generated on your device and saved with the browser's
  normal download; PDF uses the browser's own print dialog.
- **Removal:** uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
